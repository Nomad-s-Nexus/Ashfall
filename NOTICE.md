# NOTICE

**Fallout Anomaly — Radiation Overhaul** — source, plugins, and UI for the Fallout 4
radiation overhaul (F4SE C++ plugin, Papyrus, PrismaUI HUD).

## Third-party dependencies (NOT redistributed here)
The in-game storm dome reuses meshes/textures from the KORS **Anomaly** mod (Knundrum).
Those assets are required at runtime and come from that mod — they are intentionally
**not** included in this repository for licensing reasons.
