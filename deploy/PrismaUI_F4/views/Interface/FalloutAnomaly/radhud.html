Default rates, adjustable below: hunger +5%/h, thirst +10%/h, fatigue +2.5%/h, and 34/67/90% are stage 1/2/3. Rest recovers fatigue 12%/h; food relieves 20% of an item's portion, drink 25%, alcohol hydrates at 25%.

Exposure fills from ambient time, wounds, swimming, tainted food and rad weather. At 100% an illness starts, incubates, then stages up; a cure item or an uninterrupted rest clears it and grants immunity.
