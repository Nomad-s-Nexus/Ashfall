Storm Dome — Black Hole ("Event Horizon" eye)
=================================================
The black hole visualization used on the storm-dome eye is derived from a NASA
public-work visualization, used under NASA's media guidelines (credit required,
no implied endorsement):

    Credit: NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center/Jeremy Schnittman
    Source: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio #13326
            https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/13326
    Reproduction guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/multimedia/guidelines/

The image was cleaned (annotation labels inpainted out), alpha-matted, and baked
to textures/FalloutAnomaly/FODomeBlackHole.dds for use as an emissive
BSEffectShaderProperty-style card (Meshes/KORsMeshes/Anomaly/CloudArena04.nif,
spawned by FARO_StormDome04).
