# Rad Firestorm art previews

Two preview contact sheets for the Rad Firestorm weather visuals.

## Files

- `Firestorm_preview_sheet.png` (1920x1080): a 3x3 grid of nine frames labeled Horizon_005.0, Horizon_020.3, Horizon_026.0, Horizon_042.0, Horizon_070.0, Horizon_125.0, Horizon_149.0, Whirl_070.0 and Away_070.0. It shows a dark brown storm sky with a fire line along the horizon that grows from an empty sky into tall flames and a flaming whirl column, then fades back to dim smoke.
- `Firestorm_NIF_preview_sheet.png` (1920x810): a 4x3 grid of twelve frames labeled final_Horizon_008 through final_Overhead_070, including final_Horizon_020_flash, final_Horizon_033_blast, final_Horizon_140_aftermath and final_Horizon_149_clearing. It runs from a clear blue sky through the fire wall and whirl column, then aftermath and a clear sky again, plus an up view, an away view and an overhead view of dense smoke.
